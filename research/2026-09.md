## Cross-category observations
1. The frontier text leader changed from Claude Opus 5 in the August issue to Claude Opus 5.5, with an Intelligence Index of 58 in the current Artificial Analysis table.
2. September adds a broad multimodal release wave: GPT-6 Astra, Grok 4.7, MiMo-V2.6-Pro, Qwen3.8 Max, GLM-5.3 and Muse Spark 1.3 appear across current benchmark tables.
3. Media leaders are split by modality: GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst leads image, Gemini Omni Flash leads audio video, Sonic 3.6 leads TTS, and Mureka V9 leads instrumental music.
4. Open weights remain competitive but not at the absolute proprietary frontier: MiMo-V2.6-Pro is the current open-weights Intelligence leader at 46 versus 58 for Claude Opus 5.5 max.
5. Value is increasingly tiered by effort and modality: current Artificial Analysis tables expose separate max/xhigh/high/medium/low variants rather than one undifferentiated model score.
